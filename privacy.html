Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Jarvis is a personal home assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: reads the owner's calendars and adds events when he asks.
  • Gmail: reads message senders, subjects and short previews. It cannot send, delete or change mail.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's own questions. It is processed on computers in the owner's home. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage

Jarvis does not keep copies of calendar or email content. The sign-in credential is stored on a private computer in the owner's home and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through this site's GitHub repository.
